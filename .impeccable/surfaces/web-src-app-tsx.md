# Surface brief: Ferry console (web/src, all pages)

Scope and mode: the whole operator console (sign-in, Overview, Bundles, bundle and verification detail, Models, Access, Audit, Evidence). Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: enclave operators and auditors/reviewers in equal measure (PRODUCT.md). Job: confirm the enclave's state, act on what is pending (verify, import, route, key, policy), and prove what happened. Constraints: every page, route and action stays; strict CSP, offline build; modern browsers on integrated graphics; WCAG 2.2 AA; dark scene (windowless secure rooms, long sessions on plain laptops).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The console is the enclave's pre-flight checklist: every fact an operator or auditor needs is a challenge with a confirmed response, read top to bottom before anything is trusted. It refuses the category default of same-size metric cards on a grey ground with one accent.

OWN-WORLD: Dark kneeboard ground (charcoal-navy, near-black blue), a committed deeper-blue rail for navigation, bone-white type on hairline-bordered panels. Amber is the single attention colour: pending, needs attention, selection, the active checklist line. Pass green, fail red and info blue appear only where they carry state; revoked and retired items print in grey. Type: Archivo (variable, tabular numerals; caps with wide tracking for checklist section labels and stamps, regular for UI and for prose-like meta lines, times and summaries) and Azeret Mono for hashes, digests, ids, key ids, paths, URLs, model names, commands, JSON and table counters (amended 2026-10-30: B612 Mono set punctuation hard left in its cells, so versions, addresses and times read as typos). Density: 13px UI, 12px tables, 32px rows, 4/8 spacing, radius 4px. The dotted leader between an item and its stamped response is the mark that identifies the world with all content removed.

STORY: An operator signs in and reads the crossing first (where models stand on either side of the air gap), then the readiness checklist in order: confirmed, pending, failed. They act on the first pending line and watch it confirm. An auditor reads the same lines as evidence: each response carries the hash, signer or version that proves it, and one click opens the record.
