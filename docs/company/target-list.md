# Discovery target list

*Owner: founder. Built 2026-10-28 for the 15 calls in [customer-discovery.md](customer-discovery.md) (10 vendor-side, 5 receiving-side), under the positioning in [02-decision-revision](../strategy/02-decision-revision.md). This is an outreach list for discovery, not a sales database. It holds organizations and role titles only, with no contacts or names. Nobody here has been contacted, and none is a customer, pilot or partner.*

*Labels: **Evidence** cells are VERIFIED, meaning a paraphrase of the linked page as read on 2026-10-28. The bracket gives the source type, and "vendor" means the organization describing itself, not independent confirmation. **Fit notes** and **Role to approach** are INFERRED. The one exception is the Leidos role title, which appears in its source. Anything not stated on the page is marked UNKNOWN. Screens 1-4 and K4/K5 refer to customer-discovery.md §2 and §7.*

## Summary

- **38 organizations verified** against a public page: 28 vendors, 1 integrator, 3 primes, 3 labs or FFRDCs, and 3 operators. Priority: 12 A, 12 B, 12 C, 2 P (partner or competitor, not a discovery target).
- **Closest to the ICP hypothesis.** These are smaller firms whose public pages say they ship local or open-weight LLMs into air-gapped, classified or on-site settings: IESE Solutions, EdgeRunner AI, Protopia AI, Neuroscale, ibl.ai, Zylon, MetroStar, Nuclearn and Atomic Canyon. Whether each site is customer-operated and genuinely disconnected (screens 2-3) is UNKNOWN for most of them, and Neuroscale's air-gapped use is planned, not shown.
- **Receiving side with first-party evidence.** LLNL Livermore Computing runs open-weight models in restricted and secure zones, and model downloads there go through an ISSO-coordinated approval process. Army AI2C hosts open-source models on SIPRNet. IDA published a paper favouring local open-weight models for on-prem, air-gapped use, built on the open-source OnPrem.LLM toolkit (that IDA staff maintain the toolkit is INFERRED: the paper cites a toolkit paper by its own author).
- **Packaging.**
  - 9 rows state Kubernetes as their install path, and ibl.ai offers single-server or Kubernetes.
  - 6 rows name a serving stack from vLLM, Ollama, NIM, TGI or SGLang, and 2 name llama.cpp.
  - Packaging is UNKNOWN for most rows. K4 and K5 cannot be pre-judged, and vendors that publish install docs skew toward Kubernetes (INFERRED).
- **Loose "air-gapped" claims.** At least 4 rows rely partly on provider-operated environments: Sourcegraph and Leidos (AWS), Lockheed Martin (Google Distributed Cloud) and Ask Sage (IL5/IL6 cloud). Those parts fail screen 3.
- **Demo constraint.** `ferry preflight` is VERIFIED on macOS only, and Linux isolation is untested. Most targets probably serve on Linux GPU hosts (INFERRED; runtime is UNKNOWN for most rows), so test the Linux path before a demo counts (customer-discovery §8, K4).

## How this list was built and its limits

**Method.**
- Built on 2026-10-28 with about 40 web searches and 50 page reads. Each Evidence cell paraphrases its linked page as read that day. Two PDFs (Primer, Sourcegraph/Leidos) were read page by page.
- BusinessWire, GlobeNewswire and HPCwire returned HTTP 403, so syndicated copies (financialcontent, webull, Carahsoft) were used. Their text may differ from the originals.
- **Searches covered:**
  - vendor air-gap install docs
  - Tradewinds "Awardable" releases
  - sbir.gov award search: "air-gapped"; "large language model" with disconnected, classified or on-premises; "language model" offline; "small language model"; "LLM DDIL". The SBIR API returned 403.
  - national labs and FFRDCs, primes and integrators, nuclear vendors
  - channel pages
- **Inclusion rule:** a public page states that the organization delivers, deploys or runs AI or LLMs in an air-gapped, classified, disconnected or strictly on-prem setting.

**Limits.**
1. **Claims, not deployments.** About a third of rows describe a specific deployment or contract: rows 2, 3, 7, 9, 30, 32, 34, 35, 36, 37 and 38. The rest are capability claims. None was independently confirmed.
2. **"Air-gapped" is used loosely.** AWS-based (rows 26, 31), Google Distributed Cloud (row 37) and IL5/IL6 cloud (row 20) environments fail screen 3. "Classified" can also mean a provider-operated region.
3. **Unknown on most rows:**
   - Whether sites are customer-operated, and whether they are D or R (screens 2-3). Screen on the call.
   - Headcount. Only Primer's (110) is stated, so the 10-200 staff ICP band is unconfirmed elsewhere. SBIR firms are small by program rules (INFERRED).
   - Runtime and packaging. Kubernetes vendors may be over-represented because they publish install docs.
4. **Role titles are INFERRED** guesses at who owns the transfer pain, except row 31. No contact research was done: no LinkedIn, names, emails or phone numbers.
5. **Not searched:**
   - DSIP topics, SAM.gov/FPDS/USAspending awards and DIU/AFWERX award lists. Thunderforge and similar are in research/05; no air-gapped-LLM awardee turned up beyond SBIR.
   - Reddit and conference speaker lists.
   - Non-US vendors, except those encountered incidentally (Zylon, Cohere, xpander.ai; their HQs were not checked).
6. **Freshness.** Evidence ranges from May 2024 to Oct 2026. The 2024-2025 items (rows 7, 8, 9, 30, 38) may be stale.
7. **Product constraint.** Preflight is VERIFIED on macOS only; Linux isolation is untested. Before demoing to Linux-only targets, test Linux or frame the run as a joint first test (customer-discovery §8).
8. **This list is not evidence of demand.** It names who to ask; the calls test the hypotheses.
